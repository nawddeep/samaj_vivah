## 1. Directory Overview & Methodology

Following the 1947 Partition of India, the Hindu Sindhi community was entirely displaced from Sindh and resettled throughout India. To preserve their heritage, maintain community cohesion, resolve civil matters, and facilitate family matchmaking, the community established two types of mutual-aid institutions:
1. **Geographic City Panchayats & Central Councils:** Apex umbrella organizations representing all Sindhis residing within a specific modern Indian municipality (e.g., *Pujya Sindhi Central Panchayat, Jaipur*, *Pujya Sindhi Mahapanchayat, Indore*).
2. **Ancestral Biradari Panchayats:** Diaspora councils formed by families originating from specific historic towns or districts in Sindh to maintain genealogical and marriage ties (e.g., *Jacobabad Sindhi Panchayat*, *Shikarpur Sindhi Panchayat*, *Khanpur Sindhi Panchayat*).
3. **Traditional Community Lineages:** Historical socio-occupational and regional cultural identities (e.g., *Amil*, *Bhaiband*, *Sahiti*, *Larai*, *Uttaradi*, and *Radhaswami Sindhi Satsangi*).

Every record in this directory has been researched, verified against public sources, deduplicated, and assigned a stable alphanumeric identifier.

## 5. Research Gaps & Ongoing Maintenance Notes

1. **Local Camp Sub-Panchayats in Ulhasnagar:** Ulhasnagar contains micro-panchayats for Camps 1 through 5 (e.g. *O.T. Section Panchayat*, *Dussehra Maidan Samiti*). In this directory, they are consolidated under `pan_sindhu_ulh` (Sindhu Seva Samiti & Central Council, Ulhasnagar) and ancestral bodies like `pan_shikarpur_ulh` to prevent UI dropdown overload.
2. **Diaspora Organizations in UAE and North America:** International bodies (e.g. *Sindhi Association of North America - SANA*, *Indian Sindhi Association of Dubai - ISAD*) operate actively overseas. Because the current prototype is centered on national Indian alliances, international bodies are noted for future Phase 4 directory expansion.
3. **No Ambiguous "Other" Option:** As mandated by the client, users select from verified canonical Panchayats, Ancestral origins, and Traditional lineages. A dedicated unlisted-entity reporting form will be supported in Phase 2 for community review.
