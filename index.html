Try the whole loop

  1. Open the member app in one tab and sign up with any new 10-digit number (the OTP fills in by itself).
  2. Open the admin panel in another tab, go to User Approvals, and approve or reject the new member.
  3. Switch back to the member app. It updates by itself, and an approved member can browse matches.
  4. In the admin panel, hide a profile. It disappears from the member app.
